# Plan: quantity prices, production steps, and steps in jobs

Status: **planned 2026-10-16** from the founder's request, "Next, build quantity pricing and production process steps, with the production steps reflected in jobs." Four slices, one pull request each; all four change the database (**migrations, human review**). **Slices 1 to 4 are built (slices 2 and 3 together; see "Built" below).** The product form already shows "Quantity prices" and "Production steps" as "coming soon" sections (`docs/plans/products.md`); this plan makes them real.

It also takes the part of `docs/plans/costs-and-recipes.md` called "steps" (decision 2: a step can depend on a choice; slice 3: richer steps; slice 4: a job carries a frozen copy of the process). Materials as inputs, minutes, labour cost and margin stay in that plan: they need the costs work, and nothing here blocks them.

## Where things stand
- Products have a price, **variations** (each with its own price), **options** and **extras** (added amounts). A quote line keeps its **own typed price**: the item sheet starts it at the product's (or the chosen variation's) price and it "follows" that price until the maker types their own (`quotes/line-sheet.tsx`, `unitPrice`, `referencePrice`). The server stores what was typed; it does not recompute a unit price.
- Jobs hold **frozen items** (`job_items`: name, quantity, variation, options, photo; no link to the product), made from an accepted quote's sent snapshot (`create_job_from_quote`) or typed directly (`create_job`, `add_job_item`, all through `job_item_clean`). Progress is **one count per item** (`made_milli`, `set_job_item_made`); a job starts when the first unit is made and offers "Ready" when every item is made.
- A quote's sent snapshot lines do not carry the product's id (`quote_lines.product_id` exists, the snapshot drops it).

## What the maker sees
**Quantity prices (product form, replacing the "coming soon" card).** A section "Quantity prices": "Lower the price when someone orders more." with an **Add a quantity price** button (plus, then label). Each row: "From" (a quantity, with the product's unit) and "% off", with a line that reads "10 or more: 10% off (R90,00 each)" when the product has one price, and remove. Rows are kept in order of quantity; a quantity cannot be repeated.
**On a quote (item sheet).** When the quantity reaches a row, the price each drops by that percent and a line under "Price each" says "Quantity price: 10 or more: 10% off." If the maker typed their own price it is left alone, and "Use the usual price" puts the quantity price back. Changing the quantity back below the row restores the full price. Nothing is shown on the customer's quote except the lower price each.

**Production steps (product form, replacing the "coming soon" card).** A section "Production steps" (services: "Steps") with **Add a step** (plus). Each step is a name, with move up, move down and remove. Slice 4 adds "Applies to: Always / only when …". Shown for products and services alike (a service can have steps, such as "Visit" and "Write up").
**On a job.** Each product item shows its steps, in order, under the item: each step has "3 of 12" with minus, plus, a typed number and "All done". The item's own count becomes "Made 3 of 12" (the fewest step); each step has its own "All done" tick. An item without steps looks and works as it does today. The first progress on any step starts the job, and when every item is fully made the job offers "Mark ready" as now. The job shows the steps frozen as they were when it was made; changing the product later never changes a job.

## Decisions to confirm (recommendations in bold)
1. **Quantity price is a percent off.** **Recommended:** it works with variations and options and survives price changes. Alternative: a price each per row (simple for a product with one price; ambiguous with variations). The founder may want price-each for one-price products: say so and it is a small addition.
2. **The percent comes off the product or variation price only**, not options and extras. **Recommended:** options and extras are add-ons the maker priced on their own.
3. **Quantity counts the item's own quantity** (not the whole quote's). **Recommended**, as the prototype.
4. **A step is just a name (and, in slice 4, a condition).** Minutes and labour cost come with costs (`costs-and-recipes.md`). **Recommended.**
5. **A job copies steps when it is made; it never follows later changes to the product.** **Recommended** (decision 8 there).
6. **An item's made count is the fewest of its steps.** **Recommended**, as the prototype.
7. **Steps for items typed straight into a job** (not from the quote) work the same, by the product id carried in the item. A one-off item has none.
8. **The quote's sent snapshot starts carrying the product's id** (and, in slice 4, the ids of what was chosen) on each line, for versions sent from now on. Older sent versions make jobs without steps. **Recommended**; the alternative, looking the product up by the name on the line, guesses.

## Data (sketch; every table has `organisation_id`, same-business composite keys, row-level security for members, the restrictive `session_required` policy, and SQL tests)
- **Slice 1:** `product_price_breaks` (id, organisation_id, product_id, `min_quantity_milli` bigint ≥ 1, `percent_off_bp` integer 1..9999 = hundredths of a percent, unique per product and quantity). At most 10 per product. Saved with the product (`save_product_layers`, a wrapper around `save_product`, takes `p_price_breaks`: null leaves them alone, an array replaces them, like variations). Members read; writes only through that function.
- **Slice 2:** `product_steps` (id, organisation_id, product_id, name 1..120, sort_order). At most 30 per product. Saved with the product (`save_product_layers` takes `p_steps`). Slice 4 adds `applies_to_kind` / `applies_to_id` (none, a variation, an option value, or an extra; exactly one when set).
- **Slice 3:** `job_item_steps` (id, organisation_id, job_item_id, sort_order, name, `done_milli` between 0 and the item's quantity); `job_items.product_id` (nullable, a live link, like `jobs.customer_id`). `create_job_from_quote`, `create_job` and `add_job_item` copy the product's steps (the item JSON, cleaned by `job_item_clean`, carries `productId`); `set_job_step_done` (service role) sets one step's count, recomputes the item's `made_milli` as the fewest step, starts the job on first progress, and writes no event beyond the existing ones. The quote snapshot gains an optional `productId` per line.
- Members read the new tables; they cannot write them directly.
- **What is frozen:** a sent quote version's snapshot (gains `productId`, never changes after sending), and a job's steps.

## Slices
1. **Quantity prices.** Migration `product_price_breaks` and `save_product_layers`; parsing and the one function that works out a price (`lib/products/price-breaks.ts`, unit-tested: boundaries, rounding once, a variation, no breaks); the product form section; the item sheet follows quantity and shows the line. Usable on its own.
2. **Production steps on a product.** Migration `product_steps` and `save_product_layers`; the form section with the ordered list; the form shows them in order. Usable on its own (the steps are visible and ordered, even before jobs use them).

4. **Steps that depend on a choice.** Migration (`applies_to` columns), snapshot gains the ids of the variation and chosen values and extras; the form's "Applies to" control (reusing the choice pickers the product already has); steps resolved when the job is made.

**Slice 4, steps that depend on a choice (2026-10-17; founder: "build process steps that are based on a choice, could be different based on a variant, or an extra etc. I think it'll need some simple if/and/or logic")** (needs human review: migration `20261108100000_steps_by_choice.sql`: a new table with row-level security, a new column on `job_items`, a replaced `SECURITY DEFINER` trigger function, a replaced `SECURITY INVOKER` `save_product_layers`, and replaced service-role-only job functions).
- **What the maker sees:** under each step, "Only for some items" (a plus button). Each condition is "Depends on" (a variation, a choice in a list, an extra, or Quantity), then "It is" Chosen / Not chosen (for a quantity: At least / Less than, with a quantity box that shows the product's unit). With two or more conditions, "Make this step when" asks All of these are true / Any of these is true. Up to 10 conditions a step. That is the whole logic: all, any, and not; nothing nested.
- **A choice that was taken off the product:** the condition shows "A choice that was removed", and saving says so at the condition and in the summary ("This depends on a choice that was removed. Choose another, or remove this condition."). Nothing is dropped quietly.
- **Database:** `product_steps.condition_logic` ('all' or 'any'); `product_step_conditions` (kind 'chosen' names exactly one variation, list choice or extra of the same business; kind 'quantity_at_least' holds a quantity; `negate`; members manage; `session_required`; deleted with the choice it names). `job_items.chosen_ids` (the ids chosen on the item). `product_step_applies` decides one step (no conditions: always; all: every one holds; any: at least one; "not" turns one round). The copy trigger now copies only the steps that apply to the new item, with the item's quantity at that moment. `save_product_layers` takes each step as `{name, logic, conditions}` and finds a condition's choice by its position among the variations, lists and extras saved in the same call (a new choice has no id yet). `job_item_clean`, `create_job`, `add_job_item` and `create_job_from_quote` carry `chosen_ids`.
- **Quote snapshot:** sent versions now carry `chosenIds` on a product line (the variation id and each list choice and extra id). Versions sent before this carry none: a job made from one matches no "is chosen" condition, so it takes the steps without conditions, those with "not" or a quantity condition that holds, and none that need a choice.
- **Not built:** changing a step's conditions on a job that exists (a job keeps what it copied); conditions on an option typed in, or on the item's description; nested groups.
- **Known edges (review, 2026-10-17):** a quote sent before this slice has no `chosenIds`, so a job made from it cannot tell what was chosen ("is chosen" steps are skipped, "not chosen" steps are copied). A condition inserted directly can name another product's choice of the same business; it never matches, and the form then shows it as removed.

- **Counts:** a new step starts at none, so the item's count (the fewest step) follows it down; the first step on an item that was counted as a whole starts at what was made, so that count is not lost. Taking a step off makes the item's count the fewest of the steps left (unchanged when none are left). A reset brings the product's steps as they are now (honouring "only for some items" against what was chosen on the item), each at none.
- **Database:** `job_items.steps_edited`; `copy_product_steps_to_item` (the trigger's copy, now a function of its own so a reset does exactly what making the item did); `add_job_item_step`, `remove_job_item_step`, `reset_job_item_steps` (server only; the job must be Not started or Making; an item has at most 30 steps).
- **Not built:** renaming or reordering a step on one item, steps on a one-off item, a note in the job's history when steps are changed.
