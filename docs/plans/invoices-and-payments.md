# Plan: invoices and payments (thin)

Status: **planned 2026-10-17** from the founder's order of work ("costing and margins first, then thin invoices"; "you can do thin invoices after this"). Two slices, each changing the database (**money, tax documents, immutability, numbering and migrations: human review**). **Slices I1 and I2 are built** (see "Built, slice I1" and "Built, slice I2" at the end); I3 onwards is not.

It builds on `docs/plans/jobs-and-invoices.md`, Part 2 (the founder's answers there still stand: **one invoice, with the deposit shown as a payment**; invoices are the maker's choice, never automatic) and finishes what that plan sketched, now that quotes, jobs and costing exist. Where the two differ, this one wins.

## Where things stand
- **Quotes are complete up to online acceptance:** drafts, a frozen snapshot per sent version (`quote_versions.snapshot`), a real PDF drawn from it (`lib/quotes/pdf`), outcomes (accepted, declined), deposits, themes, bank details, terms, delivery or collection.
- **Costing is frozen with each sent version** (`quote_versions.costing`, private, members only), so an invoice made from a version carries the margin it was quoted at.
- **Jobs exist** (made from an accepted quote or by hand); nothing links a job to an invoice yet.
- **Numbering:** `document_sequences` already allows `invoice` and `credit_note`; `issue_document_number` is the one place numbers are given. Quotes may have gaps (not tax documents); **invoices must be gapless** and are numbered only when issued.
- **Locale:** `docs/locales/za/vat-and-documents.md` has the SARS particulars; the ZA pack has `fullInvoiceThresholdCents` (R5 000) and `missingForInvoice` (the premises address). Nothing yet has an invoice's wording.
- **Not there:** invoices, payments, credit notes, an "overdue" notion, a Documents setting for invoice wording/terms/due days.

## What the maker sees
- **From an accepted quote or its job: "Create invoice".** A sheet shows the invoice as it will be (customer, items, totals from the quote's last sent version, the deposit already asked for), with the **invoice date** (today) and the **due date** (the business's usual, changeable), a note, and a line that says what happens next: the invoice gets its number, can't be changed, and a mistake is fixed with a credit note. **Issue invoice** gives it the next number and opens it.
- **The invoice page** is the quote page's sibling: the PDF preview (the same themes and bank details), Download, the status (Unpaid, Part paid, Paid, Overdue), what is owing, the private margin ("Only you see this", from the version's frozen costing), the activity, and a link back to the quote and the job. Nothing on it edits the invoice.
- **Invoices tab/list** (More on a phone, beside Jobs' pattern): search by number or customer; filters Unpaid, Overdue, Paid; each row shows number, customer, total, owing and the status.
- **Slice I2, payments:** "Record a payment" on the invoice (amount, day, how, note); the deposit asked for on the quote can be recorded in one tap as the first payment; payments are listed, a wrong one is reversed (a new line), never edited; the status and "owing" follow. Home gets Owing and Overdue.
- **Unbuilt parts are visible placeholders:** "Adjust before issuing", "Credit note", "Email it", "Send a link they can pay". No stand-in interactions.

## Decisions (recommendations in bold)
1. **Issue straight from the accepted quote version; no editable draft in slice I1.** *Recommended.* A draft the maker adjusts needs the full item builder and a second set of tables; the quote is already edited and revised before it is accepted. Changes after issue are credit notes. (The earlier plan's draft stays as "Adjust before issuing", a visible placeholder.)
2. **Only an accepted quote can be invoiced, and from its latest sent version.** *Recommended.* A job made by hand with no quote waits for slice I3 (a direct invoice through the same item sheet).
3. **One invoice per quote by default, deposit shown as a payment** (founder 2026-10-11). *Confirmed.* A second invoice for the same quote is refused with a clear message in this slice (progress billing is a later layer). The deposit's VAT treatment is the open adviser question (`docs/locales/za/vat-and-documents.md`, answer 3: VAT may be due on receipt of a deposit); the invoice carries the quote's VAT as agreed, and the wording on the deposit is provisional.
4. **The invoice's money is the quote version's snapshot, copied, never recomputed.** *Recommended.* The totals come from the same function as the quote did, stored; the database checks nothing is made up (net + VAT = gross, lines add up) in its constraint.
5. **Status is derived, never stored or set.** *Recommended.* Unpaid / Part paid / Paid from payments; Overdue when something is owing past the due date. The list filters work from a view or from the same function.
6. **Payments are append-only; a mistake is a reversal.** *Recommended.* Slice I2.
7. **Number prefix and start under Quotes and invoices** (the page that was set up for it), with `INV-` the default; "next number" can never go back. Gapless is the database's job (the number is taken inside the issuing transaction).
8. **Due date default: 14 days** *(a suggestion)*, changeable on the invoice and as a business default (the same "your usual" pattern as the deposit).
9. **Invoice wording, per locale pack:** "Tax invoice" only for a VAT-registered business, "Invoice" otherwise (never "tax invoice" for a business that is not registered); a full invoice (R5 000 and above) needs the customer's address, and a business customer's VAT number if it has one; the abridged form leaves the customer's details and quantity out only if the pack says so. *The pack decides; the ZA rules are in `docs/locales/za/vat-and-documents.md`.* Issuing is refused with how to fix it when a required particular is missing (the business's premises address, the customer's address for a full invoice).
10. **Issued invoices never leave:** no delete grant, no update grant, a trigger that refuses changes, and `delete_account` is reworked to keep them (the test that fails the day an invoices table exists says so).
11. **No emailing and no online pay** yet (no sending domain; layer 10): Download and Share like quotes.

## Data (sketch)
Every table: `organisation_id`, same-business composite keys, row-level security for members, the restrictive `session_required` policy, SQL tests. Server-only `SECURITY DEFINER` functions do the writes (like `send_quote`), with the session and role checked.
- `invoices`: id, organisation, `number` (unique per business), `quote_id` + `quote_version` (unique: one invoice per quote in this slice), `job_id` (null), `customer_id`, `issue_date`, `due_date`, `currency_code`, `net_cents`, `vat_cents`, `gross_cents`, `snapshot` (jsonb, what the document is drawn from), `costing` (jsonb, private, copied from the version), `issued_by`, `created_at`. No update, no delete; a trigger refuses both (the one allowed change: forgetting `issued_by` when that login is deleted, as for quote versions).
- `issue_invoice(p_org, p_quote_id, p_actor, p_issue_date, p_due_date, p_snapshot, ...)`: server-only; locks the quote and the sequence; checks the quote is accepted, has a sent version and no invoice; gives the next number inside the same transaction; inserts the invoice; writes an event.
- `invoice_events` (issued; later: payment recorded, reversed): append-only.
- Slice I2: `invoice_payments` (amount_cents > 0, paid_on, method, note, `reverses_id`, created_by): append-only, never updated or deleted; the balance is the invoice total less the sum (reversals count negative); a payment cannot exceed what is owing (checked in the recording function under a lock).
- Settings: `business_profiles.invoice_due_days` (usual); invoice wording lives in the locale pack, the prefix/start in `document_sequences`.

## Built, slice I1 (2026-10-17; needs human review: migration `20261115100000_invoices.sql`, money, a tax document that must never change, gapless numbering, two new tables with RLS, a new server-only `issue_invoice`, a replaced `record_quote_outcome` and `delete_account`)
- **Database.** `invoices` and `invoice_events` (members read; no insert, update or delete grant for anyone). A trigger refuses every update and delete (the one change allowed: forgetting a deleted login, like a sent quote version); the business and customer rows cannot be deleted from under an invoice (restrict). `issue_invoice` (service role only) locks the quote, checks it is accepted, has a sent version and no invoice (one per quote), checks the document's lines, totals and version are exactly the version's and the money adds up, takes the number from `issue_document_number` in the same transaction, writes it into the document (and into the bank details' reference line when the business asks for one), copies the version's private costing, and logs the event. `record_quote_outcome` refuses to reopen a quote that has an invoice (55007). `delete_account` refuses (55007, naming the business) when the person is alone in a business that has issued invoices. Tests: `invoices.test.sql` (isolation, session required, immutability for every path, one per quote, accepted only, numbering with no gaps after refusals, costing copied and kept out of the document, server-only), `delete_account.test.sql` (reworked), and the 30-connection numbering race now also runs for invoices.
- **The document.** `QuoteSnapshot` gained an optional `invoice` block (due day, the quote's number, the labels); the PDF and the text view take their words from it (title, "Billed to", "Due date", the quote number; no "Valid until", "Needed by", "not a tax invoice" or version). `lib/invoices/snapshot.ts` builds it from the version's snapshot. The ZA locale pack holds the invoice words, the usual 14 days and the full-invoice rule.
- **Screens.** Create invoice on the accepted quote (sheet; the quote then shows where its invoice is), `/app/invoices` (list, More row, desktop sidebar), `/app/invoices/[id]` (PDF, share, summary, placeholders, margin, activity), `/app/invoices/[id]/pdf`.
- **Decisions taken (veto any):** no editable draft in I1 ("Adjust before issuing" is a visible placeholder in the sheet); due date 14 days; the deposit is not shown as a payment yet (I2), so the invoice is for the whole total; the maker's note replaces the quote's notes on the invoice; the quote's pitch and sign-off are left off an invoice; the quote's terms are kept.
- **Review follow-ups (same PR):** invoice numbering (prefix and next number, never going back) is under Quotes and invoices, beside the quote numbers, so a maker moving from another system can continue their run before the first invoice (`set_document_numbering` now takes `invoice`; credit notes still refused); an invoice is refused with a reason when the business's VAT registration changed since the quote (the amounts are the quote's); TRUNCATE is refused on the invoice tables; the sheet shows the quote's currency; INV-10000 sorts after INV-9999. The gapless claim is tested by the 30-connection race on `issue_document_number('invoice')` plus the single-connection refusal tests (rollback under load of the whole `issue_invoice` is not raced).
- **Known, accepted:** a quote's "other ways to pay" text and terms are copied onto the invoice; a rare deadlock (aborted, no wrong data) is possible between `delete_account` and a simultaneous `issue_invoice`; service-role code could insert an invoice directly (same trust boundary as quote versions).
- **Left for later in I1's area:** invoice wording/terms library; Home counts (I2).
- **Missed from the prototype:** none new.

## Built, slice I2 (2026-10-18; needs human review: migration `20261116100000_invoice_payments.sql`, money, a new table with RLS, two new server-only functions)
- **Database.** `invoice_payments`: append-only entries (amount above nothing, the day received, how, a note), written only by the server-only `record_invoice_payment` and `reverse_invoice_payment`; no grant, a trigger and a truncate guard refuse any change. The invoice's row lock serialises payments, so two at once cannot together pass the total; a payment above what is owing is refused (22003). A mistake is a **reversal**: a new entry that points at the payment (same invoice, same amount, once, and a reversal cannot be reversed: enter the payment again). Paid so far = payments - reversals; the status is worked out (Unpaid, Part paid, Paid, Overdue after the due day with something owing). Tests: `invoice_payments.test.sql` (isolation, session required, bounds, overpayment, reversal rules, append-only for every path, server-only), the invoice and delete-account tests extended.
- **Screens.** The invoice page has **Payments**: paid so far, owing, the list (reversed ones stay, marked), **Record a payment** (amount ready filled with what is owing, day not ahead of today, how it was paid optional, note), **Reverse this payment** (a short confirmation), and **Paid in full**. The **deposit** the quote asked for is offered as the first payment (**Record the deposit**: the same sheet, amount and note ready filled; it asks for the day and the way, because VAT on a deposit follows the day it is received); it goes away once anything is recorded. The invoice's PDF shows **Paid so far** and **Amount due** under the totals, drawn from the payments to date (the issued document itself never changes). **Home** shows **Money owed to you** (Owing and Overdue, each opening the Invoices list narrowed to those); the list shows what is owing on a part-paid invoice and takes `?show=unpaid|overdue|paid`.
- **Decisions taken (veto any):** the deposit is offered ready filled in, not recorded blindly on one tap (the day it was received matters for VAT); a payment can be dated up to today, never ahead; reversal is dated today and takes no note yet; the ways to pay are EFT or bank transfer, Cash, Card and Something else (in the locale pack).
- **Review follow-ups (same PR):** a trigger enforces the money rules on every insert (a reversal undoes a payment, not a reversal, for the same amount; the paid total never passes the invoice total), so even code holding the server key can't break them; Home's totals come from a small database function (`invoice_owed_totals`, runs under the person's own access) instead of loading every invoice; the PDF preview redraws after a payment; a server-side refusal moves focus to the summary and refreshes the page; the reverse buttons name their payment. Known: a part-paid invoice past its due day shows Overdue (not Part paid); Home adds amounts in the business's one currency.
- **Still later:** statements and reminders, paying online, "I've paid" from the customer, credit notes (I4), the job prompt and a direct invoice (I3). The VAT treatment of a deposit is still the open adviser question: the invoice carries the quote's VAT as agreed and the deposit is a payment against it.
- **Missed from the prototype:** none new (the prototype had no payments, only a status flip).
