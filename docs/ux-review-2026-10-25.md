# UX review, 2026-10-25: flow and consistency (second pass)

After UX passes 1 to 5 (#120 to #124). The founder asked for "another UX review for flow and consistency".

## How this was done
A scripted walk through the whole app on a phone viewport (Playwright, 55 screens): sign-up, Business profile, More, Settings, a product with an extra (each tab), materials, customers, a quote from start (item sheet, deposit, discount, save, preview, mark as sent, accept), the job (start, cancel sheet, ready, done), the invoice (create sheet, payment sheet, credit note sheet), every list, a customer's page and statement, quote and invoice settings, terms, themes, a job and an invoice with no quote, and Home before and after. Each screen was captured as a picture and as text, then read alongside the code. Two things that looked wrong in the pictures were checked and are **not** problems: the "loading" placeholders on New invoice and a customer's page were the screenshot catching the page mid-load (the pages open in 0.3 to 0.9 s on the dev server, and a customer's tabs switch in about 0.1 s).

## What the first passes fixed (holding up)
Optional sections are added and removed one way (discount, deposit, needed-by date); extras ask "How many?" plainly; settings live with their documents; cancelling a job asks first and offers Undo; one filter bar and one empty state on every list; tabs switch at once; Home tells a new business to make a quote; "without VAT" is said only to VAT-registered makers.

## N1. More than one filled button on a screen
The button hierarchy (docs/adr/0003) says one filled button per screen, for the next step. The document pages break it in every state, mostly because **Download PDF (or Share PDF) is always filled**:

| Screen | Filled buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Quote, just marked as sent | **Turn into a job**, **Download PDF** (full width) |
| Quote, accepted | **Create a job** (in the card), **Download PDF** (full width) |
| Invoice, unpaid, with a deposit asked | **Download PDF**, **Record the deposit**, **Record a payment** |
| Home, a job done and invoiced, nothing to make | **New job** (while the money owed sits lower down) |

Download PDF is full width on a quote and a small button on an invoice. **Proposed:** once a document is sent or issued, Share / Download is an outlined button (the maker already has the PDF); the filled one is the next step for that status. On an invoice with a deposit asked and nothing paid, *Record the deposit* is the filled one and *Record a payment* is outlined.

## N2. Actions that don't fit the status, each with a sentence under it
On a **sent** quote waiting for an answer, the page offers Download PDF, *Revise this quote*, the answer card (*They accepted* / *They declined* / *Withdraw this quote*) and *Quote again*. *Quote again* belongs to an answered or withdrawn quote, and *Revise* appears the moment the quote is marked sent. On an **accepted** quote, *Create invoice*, *Change the answer* and *Quote again* each carry a full sentence under the button (four sentences of help on one screen). *Change the answer* and *Quote again* use the same pink "soft" style as *They accepted*, so the positive answer and the corrections look alike.

**Proposed:** show only the actions that fit the status (sent: the answer card first, Revise second, no Quote again; accepted: job, invoice, then a quiet "More" holding Change the answer and Quote again), one line of help at most per group, and keep the soft style for one meaning.

## N3. The same form, three orders and two vocabularies
A quote, an invoice without a quote and a job without a quote ask for the same things, differently:

| | New quote | New invoice | New job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order | Customer, **About this quote**, **Dates**, Items, Delivery, Discount, Deposit, Terms, How to pay, Notes, Sign-off, Totals | Customer, **Items**, Delivery, Discount, Dates, Note, Totals | Customer, **What to make**, When and how, Private note |
| What is made | Items | Items | What to make |
| When | Dates | Dates (*Due day*) | When and how |
| Delivery choice | Delivery (you deliver, with a fee) | Delivery (you deliver, with a fee) | Delivery (you deliver) |

On a quote the items, the reason for the document, come fourth, after the intro and the dates. Dates are named four ways across the app: *Due day* (invoice form) but *Due date* on the PDF; *Day paid*, *Day they told you*, *Day of the credit note*, *Invoice date*, *Quote date*. The quote settings call it *Sign-off message*, the quote calls it *Sign-off › Message*. The new quote and new invoice show the total twice (the pinned bar and a Totals card at the end), and *Save draft* wraps onto two lines in the pinned bar.

**Proposed:** one order for all three: Customer, Items, Dates (and delivery), Money (discount, deposit), Wording (intro, terms, notes, sign-off). The intro moves down with the other wording. One word per thing: *Items*, *Dates*, *date* everywhere (*Due date*, *Date paid*), *Sign-off*. The Totals card goes; the pinned bar is the total. *Save* in the bar (it is a draft until it is sent).

## N4. Sheets end in five different ways
| Sheet | Confirm | Way out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mark this quote as sent | Mark as sent | **Cancel** |
| Cancel this job? | Cancel the job | **Keep the job** |
| Issue this invoice? (draft) | Issue invoice | **Keep editing** |
| Record a payment | **Save payment** | (only the X) |
| They accepted / They declined | **Save answer** | (only the X) |
| Create invoice, Issue a credit note, Add bank details | Issue invoice / Issue credit note / Save bank details | (only the X) |
| Item sheet | Add to quote / Save item | **Back** / **Cancel** |

Confirmations also come in three shapes: inside the page (*Remove this item?*, *Cancel this new quote?*), as a sheet (*Cancel this job?*, *Delete your account?*), or not at all. **Proposed:** every sheet ends with its verb (title *Record a payment*, button *Record payment*; *They accepted*, button *Save their answer* or *Record acceptance*) and an outlined **Cancel**; *Keep …* only where the confirm button destroys something. A confirmation that ends something (cancel a job, withdraw a quote, delete) is a sheet; taking a line off a form is the inline question.

## N5. "Private" said three ways, and they disagree
- Costs and margins: a **Private** tag, read out as "only you see this".
- A job's note: *Private note (optional)*, "Only you and your team see this."
- A customer's notes: section *Private notes*, "Anything to remember (optional). Only you and your team see this. It is never printed."

The tag says "only you"; the notes say "you and your team" (the true one: team members can see costs too). **Proposed:** one meaning, "never on anything your customer sees", one tag (*Private*) beside every such section or field, no sentence under it.

## N6. Leftovers of the optional-things rule
- **Item discount** on the item sheet is still a select starting at "No discount", while the quote's discount is `+ Add a discount`.
- **Unit (optional)** is asked on a product line that already has its unit (and on the product form it sits under the price).
- A job's item has a stepper **and** an *All made* checkbox.
- *Add steps* on a job item is a plain button without the plus (docs/adr/0008).
- The customer form hides three sections behind one *Add address, delivery details or notes* button.

## N7. Home and summaries: zeros, and money last
Home shows *0 Waiting for an answer*, *0 Drafts*, *R 0,00 Overdue*; a customer's page shows *Quotes waiting for an answer 0*, *Jobs in progress 0*. **Money owed** is the last card although it is what a maker most needs to act on. After a job is done and invoiced, the jobs card still reads "When a customer accepts a quote, turn it into a job" next to a filled **New job**.

**Proposed:** Home lists what needs attention, money first (overdue, owing), then quotes waiting for an answer, then jobs late or due this week; counts of zero are left out, and when nothing needs attention one line says so ("You're all caught up") with New quote. The same "leave out the zeros" rule on a customer's page.

## N8. The list pages' add buttons and search
Quotes: **New quote**, a plain link with no plus. Jobs and Invoices: **+ New job**, **+ New invoice**. Customers, Products and Materials: **+ Add customer / Add product / Add material**. Quotes is the only list without a search. **Proposed:** *New* for documents (quote, job, invoice), *Add* for records (customer, product, material), always with the plus; a search on Quotes like the others (customer, number).

## N9. The prompt cards
The finished job's card is titled *Invoice QT-0001?* (reads as "an invoice called QT-0001?"); its **Create invoice** is a link styled as a button, which the card's text style underlines; and it leaves the job for the quote page, where every other *Create invoice* opens its sheet in place. The logo card has *Not now* only, the job and invoice cards have *Not now* and *Don't ask me again*. **Proposed:** title *Invoice this job?*, no underline on buttons inside cards, the same two ways to dismiss on every prompt.

## Still open from the first review
- **T5, long pages with many Save buttons:** Business profile (about 3,700 px on a phone, 6 Save buttons, some full width and some not) and Quote settings (6, one of them a bare *Save*). The pricing preferences (*What your time is worth*, *The margin you aim for*) still live in the Business profile.
- **T7, "coming soon" inside real forms:** *Customer agrees to the terms* inside the quote's Terms, *Put returned items back in stock* inside the credit note sheet.
- **What you make** is asked in the Business profile and again at the top of Terms.
- The jobs filter bar scrolls the chosen chip into view and cuts *To do* in half, with no sign that it scrolls.
